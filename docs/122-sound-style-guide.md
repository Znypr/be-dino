# 122 Sound style guide and Sound Lab (BD-081, BD-343)

Written 2026-10-16. Extends the checkable rules in [110](110-sound-audit.md) section 2 and the soft-sound rule in [87](87-vision-and-feel-guide.md); the vibe words are in [114](114-handbook.md) section 0 (polished, satisfying, calm, bubbly, friendly, vibrant) and are read **per category here, not literally** (a rare hatch is loud in joy yet soft in timbre). Audience: children, parents nearby ([114](114-handbook.md) section 2). Labels: **OWNER**, **LEAD** (proposal, owner may overrule), **CODE** (what the source does), **TODO owner**. **Nobody has listened to any of these sounds** (agents cannot hear): every target below is a design target, and every loudness figure is arithmetic on cue volumes, not measured LUFS. Registry ids: [77](77-asset-registry.md) only; never invent ids.

## 1. Sound lab (CURRENT BUILD BEHAVIOR)
Settings > SOUND tab > **SOUND LAB** (`src/client/SoundLab.luau`, data in `src/shared/SoundLabData.luau`). Lists every registered sound under a category heading with a PLAY button; rows with no asset id say "no sound yet"; categories with no sound at all are listed as red TODO lines at the bottom. Plays raw (no throttle, no limiter, no ducking) at the cue's own volume x `Sfx.Master`, through the Sounds (or Weather) slider, so what you hear is the in-game level. The list is built from `Sfx.Sounds` plus `WeatherSounds.Sounds` plus the two raw HatchPresentation ids, so **a new sound shows up automatically**; `tools/verify_sound_lab.py` fails the build if a registered name has no category (add it to `SoundLabData.CategoryOf`). Not yet done: A/B/C variants and a "pick" mark (needs uploaded variants first, see section 4). Studio and phone check of the page: not done.

## 2. Global rules (LEAD, built on 110)
- **Loudness targets (design targets, not measured):** UI ticks and pickups sit quietest; rewards in the middle; rare hatches the loudest thing in the game but still soft in timbre. Cue volume before `Sfx.Master` (0.5) and sliders: ui 0.2-0.35, pickup 0.15-0.3, reveal common/uncommon 0.5-0.65, rare-up reveals 0.7-0.9 (hard clamp `Sfx.MaxCueVolume` 0.9), reward chimes 0.2-0.45, weather beds 0.15-0.3 (`WeatherSounds.MaxBed` 0.3), weather accents at most 0.5. **TODO owner:** measure real loudness (LUFS or by ear against a reference) once variants exist; target a rare fanfare about 6 dB above a common reveal, a pickup about 12 dB below a common reveal.
- **Timbre:** marimba, soft bell, bubble pop, harp, music box, wooden tick. No sharp attack (at least 10 ms fade in), no noise bursts, no sirens, little energy above about 8 kHz. Synthesized sound is allowed as audio but must be judged by ear.
- **Max 2 voices**, priority and 40% ducking as coded in `SfxRules.luau` (reveal > fusion > level > run end > shop > weather > pickup > click). Pickups and clicks are dropped, never queued. Reward cues go one at a time through `Sfx.playCue`.
- **Repeat fatigue:** 12-120 eggs a run and thousands of pickups: every repeated cue has pitch jitter (CODE: deterministic +-4%) and at least 3 variants if it fires more than once a second (TODO owner for pickup variants; 3 exist).
- **Reduce Motion / Effects Lite and Off do not mute sound**; the Sound sliders and the "Hatch sound" toggle do. A mute for each category is not built (BD-166 follow-up).
- Every sound: brief, registry line ([77](77-asset-registry.md)), owner listens, then it ships.

## 4. Making and replacing sounds (unchanged rule)
Roblox audio is uploaded by the owner through Creator Hub or Asset Manager (moderated, may have an upload allowance and delay), from the owner's own recordings or synthesis (`tools/generate_sfx.py`, regenerated softer), Creator Store audio with a checked licence, or a royalty-free or AI pack whose licence allows games ([110](110-sound-audit.md) section 3). After each upload the lead adds the [77](77-asset-registry.md) line, binds the id in `Sfx.luau` or `WeatherSounds.luau` (the lab shows it at once), runs `tools/verify_audio.py` and `tools/verify_sound_lab.py`, and the owner listens at the in-game level.
