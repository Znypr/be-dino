The root is the middle of the body, so a 14.4 long Ankylosaurus has its mouth 7.2 studs ahead of the root and a Compy 3.9; the long species walk the food under the chest before it is eaten, the short ones are closer to the mouth. Unchanged since before BD-500 but the BD-232 sizes widened the gap (Stegosaurus 7.9 to 11.7, Ankylosaurus 10.6 to 14.4 long). Options: keep (flat, simple, no species advantage) or reach = base + 0.4 x half the body length (a small bonus for long bodies; growth speed impact needs the food-per-minute measurement of BD-471).
