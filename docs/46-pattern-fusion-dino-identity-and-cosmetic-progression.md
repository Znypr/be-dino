# Pattern-specific fusion, durable dinosaur identity and per-dino cosmetics

**Owner decisions: 2026-10-08. Status: TARGET / PLANNED, NOT IN BUILD 020.** This specification covers **BD-057–BD-065**. [Task status](07-kanban.md) is canonical; current implementation is [Build 020](44-main-integration-checklist.md). Read [egg provenance and outcome contract](41-egg-outcomes-contract.md), [22-creature appearance](43-creature-runtime-integration.md), [shop/genetics spec](36-crystal-shops-egg-genetics-and-progression.md), [original layered egg art direction](39-egg-appearance-shared-vision.md) and [BD-044 egg-per-catch migration](09-reward-economy.md).

## Vision and settled owner rules

Every dinosaur is a **persisted individual**, possibly born from an egg or **crafted by consuming multiple ingredient dinosaurs**. A hatched dinosaur has an immutable original-egg record. A **fused dinosaur is a NEW collectible**, with **freshly rolled output traits informed by its ingredient traits**, a new unique ID, and immutable fusion/parent/egg lineage. Fusion must **guarantee the next stage and its permanent stat improvement**: the RNG controls *which traits it gets*, **not whether fusion succeeds**. This allows a weeks-long progression grind with suspenseful reveals while avoiding a random chance to lose ten dinosaurs for nothing. Material/gloss layers should express its new actual rolled traits, not hide them.

Owner decisions:
- **Fusion order** begins **Base → Gold → Emerald → Diamond**. More stages may be added only after playable balance/art review. The old **Base 50→Gold, Gold 50→Diamond** is historical current Build 020 behavior to be migrated, **not** the new target.
- First playable **Gold recipe costs 10 qualifying dinosaurs**, not 50, and **matches species + saved egg pattern**. Example: **ten Compies with Wavy Stripes → one Gold Wavy-Stripes Compy**. This is the owner-requested prototype threshold, **not** permission to require identical colors, conditions or Shiny to craft Gold.
- Fusion stages are the **main deterministic, earned source of permanent stat upgrades**. Lucky conditions/event traits remain special; buying cosmetics is not the primary way to become strong.
- Gold/Emerald/Diamond material/Blender effects are **additive overlays over the RESULT'S newly rolled color mix and pattern**. They must preserve legibility of those **new** genes, face and species identity and allow the **new** Shiny/event mutation (if rolled) to coexist with **separately bought new** aura/trail cosmetics. They must NOT copy any particular ingredient's traits. Compare **subtle and medium** surface treatments.
- Auras/trails can be purchased repeatedly **for separate dinosaur instances**. A living dino can own several looks and freely switch among them. **On fusion, ALL consumed dinos' owned and equipped auras/trails are LOST; none transfers to the new fused dinosaur**, which starts with no paid cosmetics. Show an explicit irreversible-loss warning, particularly for premium/purchased or equipped looks; determine refund/retention policy for legacy paid items before deployment.
- The **Fusion Menu** must filter/sort individuals by **species, stage, egg pattern, color, condition, BIG/Shiny, mutation, rarity, origin and equipped/owned cosmetics**. It needs a selectable ingredient grid with chosen 0/10 slots, eligible/ineligible reasons, protected item confirmations, computed pre-fusion odds and clear consumption warnings. **Dino Details** must distinguish a hatched dino's original egg from a fused dino's **list of parent dinosaurs and original eggs**. Bought cosmetics never influence rarity.
- **Rarity truth:** fusion stage is a guaranteed crafted achievement; the output's **color/Shiny/mutation/other randomized fusion traits** can have **a real conditional probability for the selected ingredient set**. Keep **original natural hatch rarity** for egg-born individuals distinct from **crafted fusion result odds** and earned fusion-stage prestige. Do NOT multiply an imaginary “Gold hatch %” or any purchased cosmetics into the number.

## 1. Tier ladder and recipe decisions — BD-057

**Confirmed order and first recipe:** Base → Gold → Emerald → Diamond; Gold uses 10 same-species, same-pattern dinosaurs in the first prototype.

**Suggested future extension for exploration**, not approved tiers or shipping promise:

| Order | Stage | Visual identity | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Base | Natural egg genetics and species pattern | Hatch baseline |
| 1 | **Gold** | Warm metallic highlights, selective glossy trim following scales/pattern contours | First recognizable targeted grind |
| 2 | **Emerald** | Deep green gemstone edges, restrained crystalline facets, bright specular accents | Midgame identity; comes before Diamond |
| 3 | **Diamond** | Cool opalescent glass/crystal facets, high-frequency highlights, patterned refractions | Major late-game milestone |
| 4 | *Obsidian* | Dark glass with selective iridescent fracture seams and gold/teal edges | Optional later extension; avoid turning underlying colors black |
| 5 | *Celestial* | Polished antique iridescent mineral, tiny luminous etched relief and restrained star contours | Optional endgame extension; keep separate from **Astra condition** and **Astra trail** |

**Pattern-specific branches (still current owner direction):** the first Gold recipe requires **same species + same canonical saved pattern** on all ten ingredients. Thus a fusion of ten Wavy-Stripes Compies gives a **Wavy-Stripes Gold Compy** with **pattern fixed by recipe**, but freshly rolled color/coverage, Shiny, condition, BIG and event mutation according to explicit ingredient-aware rules. The wheel must NOT pretend to randomly choose a pattern when the recipe has only one possible pattern; use a short **“PATTERN LOCKED: WAVY STRIPES”** reveal instead. A real pattern roulette is possible **only if a future recipe actually permits mixed-pattern donors**, a separate unapproved design. Canonical pattern IDs remain unchanged; do not make identical colors a hidden requirement.

**Fusion ingredient and output lifecycle, first prototype:**
1. Open the **Fusion Menu**, choose a **target stage** (Base → Gold, Gold → Emerald or Emerald → Diamond), then filter down to **same eligible species and pattern**. Select **10 actual Base instances for Gold**, each with its **own source egg, genome, cosmetic inventory and unique ID**. All 10, including any highlighted “lead ingredient,” are **consumed** on success; the server creates **one new Gold instance**. There is **no hero dinosaur that survives unchanged**.
2. The player can deliberately select Shiny, mutated, rare-color and other premium donors **because their traits affect the result**. Mark valuable, favorited, locked or currently equipped dinos clearly. Default auto-fill chooses only ordinary eligible, unprotected copies; locked/rare/purchased-cosmetic-bearing donors require explicit manual selection and a separate irreversible confirmation. Do not silently spend special copies.
3. Show an **ingredient preview and dynamic trait-odds panel** for the exact ten selected instances; if an ingredient changes, recompute all outcome odds on the server. The player's selected ingredients, their *entire saved trait payloads*, recipe and outcome-table version are frozen in a server-validated fusion quote. No client RNG determines the reward.
4. On confirm, atomically **consume all ingredient instances**, settle the **guaranteed upgrade stage and stats**, and **save/commit one output with new ID, newly rolled genetics/traits and the full ingredient lineage**. Retry/rejoin shows the same committed result; no second deduction or reroll. Client wheels are **only a timed reveal of the committed server outcome**.
5. **Colors, condition, Shiny, mutation and BIG are output-rolls**, not preserved verbatim from any particular ingredient. **Pattern is deterministic for the current same-pattern recipe**. Each ingredient contributes via the published formula, with independent/correlated outcome factors disclosed accurately. The precise color/condition/BIG rules and mutation selection weights still need balance approval; do not invent a random chance of fusion failure.
6. **All auras/trails bought for consumed ingredients are destroyed on fusion, including the chosen lead ingredient.** No free cosmetic ownership moves to the newly crafted result. Show clear “X auras and Y trails will be lost” confirmation with product names. For purchases sourced from Robux/premium currencies, migration, player expectation and potential compensation must be explicitly resolved before release; do not quietly delete pre-existing paid assets.
7. Gold → Emerald → Diamond still require **pattern-matched prior-tier ingredients** if those branches are retained, but costs must be simulated. Ten per tier would make one Diamond cost ~1,000 patterned Base dinos. The earlier **3 Gold → Emerald / 2 Emerald → Diamond** plan remains an **unapproved example**, not a finalized recipe.
8. **Guaranteed stage/strength progression** must remain deterministic while cosmetic/natural trait rolls provide entertaining suspense. The player always knows exact consumed costs and next stage. Additional Obsidian/Celestial stages, optional trait materials and the deterministic pattern-research system require separate approval.

### Quantitative feasibility checkpoint (base odds only)

Source-verified Build 020 **purchased/one-egg base species weights** allocate **Compy 25 out of 100** and the current eight pattern IDs are selected uniformly. Under the new **50% Cracked** condition selection and preserved **20% cracked hatch success**, average hatch success at tier 0 is **60%**. The current per-egg 1/2/3 copy mean is **1.35 copies** (70/25/5 distribution). These independent prototype baselines imply `0.25 * (1/8) * 0.60 * 1.35 = 0.0253125` eligible striped Compy copies per egg, or **~2.53 copies per 100 eggs**, **~395 eggs expected for 10**, approximately **26.3 runs if and only if each run earns 15 eggs**. This is **NOT current-run measured odds**: 15-egg batch rare-species boosts lower Compy's share, upgrade tiers/weather change condition/rarity, protection/fodder choices change eligible consumption, and the BD-044 15-egg run system is not implemented. The expected count is *not* a guarantee or a pacing promise. Record an actual simulation across event mixes and account profiles before locking recipes.

**Weeks-long retention without punishing grind:** Combine guaranteed higher-stage results with donor-weighted trait rolls, visible donor progress 0/10, repeatable species/pattern mastery, earned seasonal/event objectives and eventual endgame optional cosmetics. Not every rare gene/condition should be mandatory fusion fodder. Use measured **successful eggs/hour, target species+pattern/hour, acquisition after Cracked failures, runs/day, weeks to each stage, PvP fairness and speed cap** to tune costs. Provide goals at short session, few-day and multi-week horizons. No daily login mandate or speculative odds/power promises. Demonstrate casual and heavy play paths using 15-/150-egg sample runs (not guaranteed per five minutes). Keep playability without Robux and high-priced crystals.

## Targeted pattern research / duplicate sink — BD-063 (proposal, not approved mechanic)

**Problem:** even though fusion itself is deterministic, a ten-*exact-pattern* recipe remains primarily RNG-gated. Earning hundreds of eggs for a single common patterned target can feel unrewarding, especially with the new condition-failure rate. Provide a **nonrandom, finite, earned-only alternative** after measuring how many correct-pattern eggs players actually receive.

**Candidate options to prototype and compare** (select one with owner before implementation):
- **Same-species Pattern Research:** unwanted same-species patterns can fill a persistent, fixed-rate **research bar** for a chosen species/pattern and unlock a separate earned milestone, recipe discovery, or non-donor crafting resource. It does **not** generate pretend Shiny/colored dinosaurs or replace any of the ten real donors under the currently requested recipe. The goal is another deterministic progress avenue, not a false trait donor. No Robux instant skip.
- **Fixed-rate duplicate salvage:** trade a fixed number of ordinary nonmatching same-species spares for a **species-pattern research material** used in separate future mastery goals (not one of the ten trait-bearing donors). Show exact before/after quantities and never auto-burn protected/rare/paid-cosmetic dinos.
- **Species/pattern mastery objectives:** deterministic objectives for actually playing with a species, collecting food/PvP milestones or finding patterns; rewards provide progress for future milestones or clearly labeled non-donor material sinks, not fictional inherited traits.

**Fusion eligibility stays honest:** the 10-copy Gold prototype requires **10 eligible real matching-pattern dinosaur instances** with source traits that feed result odds. **Pattern research vouchers are a separate future proposal, not valid current ingredients.** A credit has no Shiny, color, condition or mutation genome and **must never receive fake traits or secretly count as a donor**. If vouchers are later allowed, define a separate material slot, explicit odds semantics and no-gene denominator, and show the precise input mix. Until then research may unlock a future deterministic recipe discount instead of substituting for trait-bearing inputs.

**Required abuse protections:** species/pattern-bound currency; server-only atomic granting/consumption; account XP/level integration without pay shortcuts; rate caps on PvP/repeat-victim farming; safe migration, expiry rules if any, no value inflation from 1/2/3 copies or a single egg; UI progress always visible and testable after reconnect. Separate earned material credits from random egg payout/disclosure. Measure casual and regular players' time to desired variants, not just fastest farm route.

## 2. Stats and meaningful deterministic progress — BD-058

- A fusion stage supplies a **server-authoritative permanent movement speed, food-growth efficiency and/or non-PvP ability modifier**, with a clear before/after comparison; start from conservative **illustrative** bonuses (not owner-locked), apply existing condition penalties, weather/mutation/potion interactions and hard caps in `ProgressionConfig`. Current safety starting caps are **2.2x speed / 8x growth**.
- **Equipped cosmetic appearance must not force a player to use a visually disliked aura/trail for maximum stats.** Existing Build 020 auras grant growth bonuses and trails grant speed. As the owner shifts primary power to fusion, evaluate either **purely visual appearance selection** with fusion as the main stat source or **unlock-based perks independent of the selected visual skin**. Don't silently delete already paid-for benefits or abruptly nerf saved accounts; define an additive migration/compensation policy first.
- Stat mods are based on the **newly created fusion-output instance's guaranteed stage** and **its freshly rolled fusion traits**, not a universal account-wide multiplier or one preserved donor genome. Independently track persistent account level requirements from trail-shop catalog so it does not accidentally transfer cosmetics. Keep PvP and run matching fair and measurable at all stages; avoid paid purchase becoming a direct shortcut to the main fusion booster.
- Include a predictable stat formula/stacking order (base species, condition, earned stage, event mutation, potion/weather and any explicitly approved unlocked passive; final clamp) and bound movement validation so a mutated BIG high-stage dinosaur cannot outrun anti-cheat or clip impossible slopes. BIG remains size-only.

## 3. Per-dino aura/trail inventory and purchase rules — BD-059

- **Recipient is a stable dinosaur instance**, not just `speciesId`, `patternId`, account-wide `ownedAuras` or the global equipped species. Two distinct Compies must be allowed to own different purchases, even if genetically identical. Each instance stores **ownedAuraIds set**, **ownedTrailIds set**, `equippedAuraId?` and `equippedTrailId?`; no transfer between dino instances and no account-wide unlock by default.
- Buying **Fern Drift for Compy A** does **not** grant Fern to Compy B. The owner can later also buy Tidal Wake for Compy A, and freely switch A between Fern and Tidal or None/Normal White without paying again. B can buy Fern separately at the usual price. Same item re-purchase on the *same* dino should be idempotently rejected or labelled Owned, not charge twice.
- Shop clearly shows the currently targeted/equipped dinosaur's **picture/name/instance identity**, with “Buy for this Dino” and recipient pinned in server purchase request + confirmation. If the player switches dino while the shop is open, require refresh/re-confirm. Server rejects stale recipient IDs, non-owned recipient and inventory/price/level-gate bypass.
- On fusion, **every input instance is consumed**. **None of its bought/equipped aura or trail licenses transfers to the newly created fusion output**, even for a visually highlighted primary ingredient. The new fused dinosaur may buy entirely new aura/trail choices and later change among those purchases until that dinosaur itself is fused. Automatically protect ingredient instances that own purchased looks; if manually included, display a strong **itemized loss confirmation**. Premium-currency purchase rights/legacy migration and refund policy require owner and compliance review before enabling destructive fusion. Do not silently refund, relocate or erase existing users' purchases.
- Preserve existing global-account catalog ownership and equip values through a **documented migration**: old unlocks can be kept in a nontransferable legacy grant or assigned to the currently equipped hero with explicit owner-visible disclosure; neither retroactively confiscate purchases nor grant unlimited items to all dinos by accident. Save schema version, idempotent ownership transactions, session lock/retry, remote bounds and reconnect. Default-free trail semantics remain free if approved.
- In detail/pick menu, display **currently equipped** aura/trail, all **owned alternatives** and their rarity, plus None and intentional visual downgrades. Shop and collection use the same per-dino inventory source and do not fake shared account ownership.

## 4. Stable copy identity, original egg and lifecycle — BD-060

**This is a prerequisite for BD-057/059/061.** Existing Build 020 stores grouped count/variant progression and equipped species; the earliest profile contract stored `collection[speciesId].base/gold` and catalog equipment. Direct per-instance cosmetic allocation and origin history must not be bolted onto one species count without an additive migration.

Suggested durable model (schema illustration, not final serializer):

```text
DinoInstance {
  instanceId: server UUID / bounded unique id; ownerId; speciesId;
  currentFusionStage; fusionBranchPatternId; favorited/locked;
  origin { kind: "hatch" | "fusion",
           // hatch: immutable eggId, source, egg visual and odds version;
           // fusion: fusionOperationId, stage recipe/version, parentIds,
           // bounded parent egg lineage refs/snapshots, selected-input digest };
  currentGenes { conditionId, color1, color2, share, patternId,
                 big, shiny, eventMutationId?, traitRollVersion };
  rolledFusionOdds?;
  ownedAuraIds: validated deduplicated set; ownedTrailIds: set;
  equippedAuraId?; equippedTrailId?;
  currentStats; displayVersion; ...
}
```

- **Original hatch egg is immutable** for each hatched ingredient: retain a compact source/versioned snapshot sufficient for the picker. If one egg produces 2–3 copies, create distinct IDs referencing the same immutable egg source. **A fused dinosaur is never represented as “hatched from” one ingredient's egg**. Its origin says **“Fused from 10 dinos”** with a browsable, bounded ingredient tree/list of **parent egg records**, the committed recipe and new resultant traits. It must not invent a new hatch egg or pretend donor genes survived. Avoid exponential ancestry storage: use stable bounded immutable transaction records/summaries and safe archived placeholders for deleted ancestor instances.
- Legacy grouped stacks need an **efficient hybrid batch+unique-instance strategy** to avoid expensive full per-egg data for 150+ egg run batches and 22 species. Keep lazy materialization/stack splitting when a dino is equipped, fused, protected or customized; preserve copy counts and variant keys; don't manufacture unique historical egg origins for old saves. Show **“Legacy origin unavailable”** with safe default/approximate preview for older collection copies.
- Avoid UUID collisions, cross-account aliasing, inventory bloat/DataStore budget violations, variant merging across genotypes, double claims, stale writer races or accidental cosmetic grants. Compression/splitting and transaction versioning must be testable and reversible without changing actual published profiles during development.

## 5. Modular premium appearance, Blender deliverables — BD-057 + BD-061

**Art pipeline:** authored **Blender source models, surface masks, normal/roughness/metalness texture treatments and optional small accessory meshes**, not brute-force duplicating all 22 species × 8 patterns × 9 color families × stages × mutations. Same rig/silhouette/camera rules across tiers. Roblox implementation may require baked composite texture layers, authored SurfaceAppearance templates and/or small rig-attached accents; confirm what the actual editable runtime renderer supports rather than assuming arbitrary alpha-stacked PBR layers. Prevent duplicate overlay mesh z-fighting and material loss from runtime gene recoloring. Do not overwrite the underlying saved gene texture.

**Two mandatory A/B art variants, each stage:**

| Surface level | Design goal | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subtle** | Recognizable metallic/mineral gloss and selective species-anatomy accents; texture and colored pattern remain dominant | The egg's stripe/spot pattern and both colors are legible at a glance |
| **Medium** | More complex polished trim, gemstone facet relief, additional highlights along pattern boundaries, horn/claw/ridge accents | Higher prestige without solid covering or washing out DNA/patterns |

**Suggested additive stack for the NEW output:** authored base rig/face/shape → **freshly rolled** two-gene color coverage → fixed recipe pattern (or future legitimately rolled pattern) → **new** condition treatment → guaranteed Gold/Emerald/Diamond **surface material/mask/detail** → **newly rolled** event mutation smoke/wisps → **newly rolled** Shiny glints → **separately repurchased** output aura orbit → **separately repurchased** output trail. BIG scales the coordinated assembly without changing stats. Render the output with **no aura/trail by default** and with newly purchased ones in stack tests; neither donor's aura/trail survives.

**Testing matrix at minimum:** six original + selected new dino body types (quadruped/horned, feathered, large long-neck, pterosaur, aquatic); each with stripes, spots and blank; contrasting and nearly black/monochrome color genes; Gold/Emerald/Diamond with **Subtle and Medium**; normal vs BIG; Shiny on/off; event mutation ember/frost/aurora; light/dark ambient weather; with/without aura and trail; running/still, world/camera/UI previews, at default and max growth. Prioritize readability and silhouette, masking/UV consistency, render order, particle clutter, UI crop, skin-tone conservation, phone performance, reduced effects. Visually compare **Base vs Gold vs Emerald vs Diamond** using the *same identical* color/pattern/genome so fusion improvements are measurable rather than random dino differences.

Art sources/assets required: versioned .blend masters (not only PNG screenshots), material-node libraries/masks, exportable optimized meshes or reusable accent meshes, original transparent preview PNGs, manifest with logical IDs/owner-bound uploaded IDs, screenshots/short videos and measurable draw-call/triangle/particle costs. **No final overlay choice approved until owner reviews subtle vs medium stacks.** Consider a user-adjustable effect-intensity slider only after measuring if it is affordable (optional, not requested).

## 6. Dino picker, egg origin and truthful rarity — BD-062

**Information architecture:** compact species/variant cards may show fusion-stage border, egg pattern mark, natural/conditional rarity badge, BIG/Shiny, mutation badge and equipped aura/trail icons. Open **Dino Details** for full *current* traits, two color genes and percentage, **hatch egg or fusion lineage**, condition, acquired status, previous fusion ingredients/recipe, rarity breakdown, **only its own newly bought** equipped/owned cosmetics and stats. Avoid trying to fit every label on a touch-sized grid. The small cards must expose essential distinguishing traits without 12 competing badges; tooltips and accessible text labels rather than color-only coding.

**Two separate measures:**
1. **Natural hatch rarity for HATCHED dinos**, a mathematically defensible percentage/“1 in X” if and only if derived from the **correct versioned source and conditional distributions** for species, pattern, two gene colors and coverage, successful condition, BIG/Shiny, event mutation given its event/time, etc. **For FUSED dinos, show “Fusion result odds: X% given these specific input dinosaurs”** using the committed ingredient-dependent roll table instead of calling it a naturally hatched probability. *Do not* multiply marginal chances as though correlated conditions/events were independent. Account for failed Cracked outcomes and paid vs free egg sources, and don't compare different event contexts without labeling them. For combinations not calculable from preserved versions, show **Unknown / estimated**, not a made-up numeric probability.
2. **Total earned rarity/prestige**, which includes *current actual* natural/fused traits **plus the guaranteed crafted fusion stage** and other earned, nonpurchased progression; explicitly label the stage **Crafted**, and separately label the true **conditional fusion-trait outcome odds** if calculable. **Exclude** purchased trails/auras, their prices, player Robux spent and current equipped cosmetic selection. If a single combined number is desired, use a **separately defined index or descriptive tier** with a published score methodology, **not** an invented joint random percentage. E.g., `Natural: 1 in X conditional on Rain egg; Crafted: Emerald Wavy-Stripes Compy; Overall prestige: [earned tier]`.

**Rarity UI example:**
- **Emerald Wavy-Stripes Compy** / BIG / Shiny / Ember, **NEWLY ROLLED** color genes Green 20% • Blue 80%. Origin panel says **“Fused from 3 Gold Wavy-Stripes Compies”** (example higher-stage recipe), with parent egg thumbnail history rather than falsely claiming this new dinosaur hatched from one egg. Show **“Fusion result: X% conditional on selected donors”** only if the saved odds version can calculate it. `Fusion: Emerald (guaranteed stage)`.
- Right after fusion: `Aura: None (purchase for this new dino)`; `Trail: Free/default only`. After separately purchasing Meadow/Fern/Tidal on this new dino, the player may switch among its own bought styles. Their purchased rarity labels are **never** included in natural/fusion/earned rarity.
- Tap to view **source ingredient contributions**, input Shiny count and mutation types, confirmed recipe, actual precommitted trait odds and resultant stage/stat values. A combined `0.0005%` must be restricted to a real **conditional exact fusion-result distribution**, never the crafted stage multiplied by a source egg's probability.

**UI/QA:** sorting/filter by species, pattern, stage, natural traits, origin egg and equipped effects; no merging visually distinct egg genotypes; count verification across old stacks, 1/2/3-copy hatches and new 150-egg batches; full mobile/tablet/desktop controls and 8–12 player performance. When two dinos have identical species/pattern but different origin/purchases, both must remain individually selectable. Unavailable legacy original egg must show a truthful fallback without inventing traits.

## 7. Explicit fusion RNG design — BD-064

**Owner direction:** ingredient trait makeup affects the probability of the NEW fused dinosaur. A **2 of 10 Shiny donors → 10% Shiny chance** example was requested. The raw fraction of Shiny donors is **20%**, so a reduction coefficient or different weighting is necessary. **Proposed starting formula**, subject to test/owner approval:

```text
P(fused Shiny | donors) = 0.5 × (number of Shiny donors / total donors)
```

For exactly **10 ingredients**: 0 Shiny → **0%**, 1 → **5%**, 2 → **10%**, 5 → **25%**, 10 → **50%**. This is **not** a guaranteed 10% result, and sacrificing ten Shiny dinosaurs for a maximum 50% roll could feel punishing. Test donor value, trait recycling, guaranteed mastery alternatives and actual player fun before locking this coefficient. At higher-tier 3/2 recipes, use donor-fraction logic if chosen; don't blindly use 5% per ingredient at every stage. Baseline chance for 0 Shiny inputs is provisionally **0%** until a distinct fusion baseline is approved.

**Mutation wheel proposal:** for each existing mutation identity, choose a distinct weighted probability from selected parents, e.g., `P(output Ember)=0.5 × Ember_donors / N`, `P(output Frost)=0.5 × Frost_donors / N`; `P(No event mutation)=1−sum(P(each mutation))`. Since each donor has at most one mutation, the probabilities sum to at most 50%, so multiple mutations **never accidentally stack** unless a separately approved multi-mutation mechanic exists. This is only a candidate model, not owner-approved numerics. Mutated donors can be intentionally selected and sacrificed; show actual Ember/Frost/None odds in the UI.

**Color wheel proposal:** each input contributes its two saved gene colors and stored proportions to a **versioned, published ingredient-color pool**. Roll two output color genes and a new output coverage (or roll from explicitly weighted parental blends). Preserve legitimate exceptionally rare colors without inventing pure Black gene results from 0% ancestry. Matching output genes use coherent tonal contrast; exact gene-sampling and 1–99 coverage distributions require balancing and exhaustive tests. The result is not a copied “lead donor” genome.

**Condition wheel:** roll one output condition using a defined input-weighting rule; its *stat effect* is applied to the fused dinosaur. A Cracked output does **not** re-run the source egg's 80% hatch-failure roll: the fusion never fails or deletes the earned next-tier result. **BIG wheel:** independent candidate based on selected BIG counts, with separate coefficient/baseline to decide. **Pattern** is a locked recipe identity for now; do not animate a dishonest random choice.

**Server integrity:** prepare a snapshot of exact donor IDs/versions/traits, recipe, player eligibility, result probabilities, source currencies/paid-random classification and input loss. Before client animation, atomically consume the donors and commit a new dinosaur with precomputed output traits, fusion operation ID, outcome version and bounded lineage. The wheel only **reveals** saved outcomes; skipping/closing/latency/rejoin cannot reroll, charge again or modify odds. Publish inspectable real numbers for possible final outcomes. Never render a fake near-miss or manipulate displayed speed to imply different chances.

**Paid random-item gate:** Roblox's [paid-random-item rules](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items) explicitly cover randomized **combination/synthesis** outcomes and items purchased **indirectly** with Robux. Because eggs, currencies and items may have Robux-linked origins, **do not assert every fusion is automatically free/ungated**. Trace source eligibility; disclose final numerical outcome odds before consumption; use `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync` restrictions and offer an eligible earned-only or nonrandom path where appropriate. Keep existing paid-random release flags **disabled** pending actual policy and Studio tests. Product rights/refund decisions for consumed per-dino cosmetics require separate review.

## 8. Dedicated Fusion Menu and staged reveal — BD-065

1. **Entry:** a dedicated **FUSION** menu with target `Gold / Emerald / Diamond` tabs, current recipe/status/progress and a compact hero preview. Clearly distinguish *what gets upgraded* (guaranteed tier/stats) from *what gets rolled* (traits). Keep established fusion entry/icon where useful.
2. **Browse:** a grid of **INDIVIDUAL dinos**, not one stacked species thumbnail. Filtering: stage/base vs Gold, **species**, matching **pattern**, condition, primary/secondary color and blend, **Shiny** yes/no, mutation type, **BIG**, rarity, source-egg context, **has purchased aura/trail**, locked/favorited and equipped. Sorting: most common/rare, rarity, copies, Shiny, mutation, color, recent, eligible, selected. For same-species recipes, default to the recipe's species; show all matching-pattern candidates, and disable wrong-stage/wrong-pattern cards with explanations.
3. **Ten ingredient slots:** tap to select/deselect, show selected count e.g. `7/10`, source-egg and individual distinctions, trait histogram `2 Shiny / 10`, inherited weighted mutation breakdown, remaining missing copies. An optional **auto-fill plain donors only** never silently adds Shiny, mutated, paid-cosmetic-bearing or protected dinos. Explicit controls let the player purposely select valuable Shiny/mutated ingredients to improve trait chances.
4. **Probability preview:** live recalculation for exact selected ingredient snapshot, such as `Shiny: 10%`, `Ember: X%`, `Frost: Y%`, `No mutation: Z%`, color and condition details. Call out proposed vs implemented weights honestly. Never let the player's last action after preview silently change the odds; require refresh and re-confirmation if any source changed.
5. **Consent/commit:** confirmation screen lists **all donors that will be consumed**, new guaranteed fusion tier/stat improvement, exact Shiny and mutation/other rolled outcome odds, and **all bought auras/trails that will be destroyed**. Second explicit confirmation for rare/favorited/paid cosmetic donors; no undo after atomic commit. Cancelling before server commit consumes nothing.
