Be Dino! hatch, storyboard v2

From 4 top-game videos (Pet Simulator 99, Adopt Me, Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity): one big hero egg, a build-up that grows with rarity, a white flash before every reveal, shell shards bursting out, the pet huge with its name banner above, bigger shows for rarer eggs (PS99 rare = a dark cut-scene with lightning). Ours adds what they don't have: paint buckets that splash the egg's real colours and pattern on it. Full screen, kit buttons. Paint, cracks, shards and buckets here are sketches of the beats; the game gets Blender / generated art.
