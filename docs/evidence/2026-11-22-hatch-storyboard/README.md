1. Egg and dino too small: egg 35-45 % of screen height, dino 40-50 %, background darkened and blurred.
2. Not separate from play: hide the HUD; its own stage with the striped studded header.
3. Colour / pattern reveals are tiny labels: the splash covers about half the egg; a big name + rarity chip for about a second.
4. No suspense: a short shake / crack build-up with rising glow before the reveal.
5. Legendary looks like Common: its own 1.5-2 s gold build-up, shake, flash, confetti, big banner, sound.
6. Getting the dino doesn't feel like a reward: NEW!, big rarity banner, colour + pattern chips, a short entrance.
7. No payoff at the end of a batch: a summary with the best find 1.5-2x bigger, PLAY AGAIN and HOME.
8. Long batches get slow: quick hatch groups repeated Commons into cards, and always stops for a special find.
