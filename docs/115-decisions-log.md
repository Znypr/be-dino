## 2026-10-16 (fused tier look, Gold/Emerald/Diamond)
- OWNER 2026-10-16: a fused tier keeps the dino's EXACT colours and pattern, covered by a semi-translucent, glossy tier coat (Gold: warm amber/gold; Emerald: green gem with mint glow; Diamond: icy blue-white crystal), horns and claws fully in the tier colour, plus a bright tier-coloured outline with colour bleeding inward from the edge. - OWNER 2026-10-16: tier-look tuning is done in a VFX test place only, with Realistic (Future) lighting, compared under identical bright daylight; the live game is unchanged until the owner approves the look.
- LEAD findings 2026-10-16 (verified with control tests in Studio, docs/evidence/2026-10-16-tiers/):
  - A SurfaceAppearance created by a script at runtime does NOT render (EditableImage content or asset ids alike). One authored in Edit mode (template in ReplicatedStorage) and cloned at runtime DOES render.
  - Dino stripes are runtime-baked EditableImages, so PBR maps cannot sit on the stripe surface. The tier coat is a separate, slightly inflated shell with an alpha-mapped SurfaceAppearance over the stripes ("approach C"). A plain transparent shell without maps washes the stripes out; a solid single mesh keeps stripes but cannot shine.
  - At graphics quality 1, metal/foil reflections are off entirely, so the tint and outline must still read as the tier at low quality (phones).
  - Test maps were uploaded to the owner's account with the owner's OK (ids in the lead's notes; one test metal map failed to fetch).
