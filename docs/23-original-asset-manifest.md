## Visual direction

- **Compy:** slim green body, long tail, amber crest/back spikes and oversized eyes.
- **Triceratops:** squat blue body, large dark frill, cream horns and four-legged silhouette.
- **T-Rex:** rust body, oversized head/jaw, tiny arms, strong thighs and neck ridges.
- **Gold:** shared metallic amber treatment with one light badge; no excessive particles or bloom.
- **Arena:** warm meadow, sandstone starter nest, slate obstacle rocks, rounded shrubs/trees and a small bone patch.

All decorative environment pieces are non-colliding. Only the ground, boundaries, obstacle lane and slope participate in collision.
