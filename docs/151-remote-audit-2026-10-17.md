# 151 Remote and server-entry audit, as an exploiter sees it (BD-608, 2026-10-17)

Label: **CURRENT BUILD BEHAVIOR** audit of main `18a9d665` (after about 80 PRs on 2026-10-17), **offline only**. The real `src/server` code ran inside the Luau test harness with a mocked DataStore (latency, the per-server request budget, failed writes, lost acknowledgements). Nothing was run in Studio, on a published server or on a phone, and nothing here is a statement about Roblox networking, client physics or real DataStore timing. Merged with the ProfileRepository split ([150](150-profile-repository-split.md), BD-607) on 2026-10-18: the repository functions keep their names, their files changed (`ProfileEggs`, `ProfileShop`, `ProfileCollection`, `ProfileFusion`, `ProfileSkins`, ...). Sources read: [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), [145](145-session1-readiness-2026-10-17.md), [55 s8](55-playtest-release-runbook.md) (rollback), [111](111-multiplayer-audit.md), [112](112-private-state.md), [13](13-persistence-remote-contracts.md), [22](22-security-multiplayer-test.md), and every file in `src/server`.

## 1. Result in one paragraph

**No path was found that gives a player free currency, items or another player's data.** Every one of the 29 remote handlers (18 on a published playtest server, 11 more behind flags that are off or Studio only), the receipt callback and the pedestal prompt validates its arguments, takes rewards, ids and time from the server (a character's position is the client's: F1), and ends in an idempotent, lease-checked transaction; 2,255 slot-by-slot hostile calls, 51,425 hostile argument pairs, 40-way parallel replays, lost acknowledgements, three random 260-request sequences on a failing store and floods from up to four clients produced **no error, no corrupted profile and no value created**. What the audit did find is the layer around that: **3 P1** (movement checks accept 3 to 5 times normal speed; a purchase of a Robux crystal pack is honoured even though the packs are switched off; a dinosaur can be swapped in the middle of a run), **4 P2** (a few hostile clients can make other players' routine writes fail; empty runs cost a write each; two objective rewards need no real action; the old 50-copy mutation is still live) and a **P3** bundle. **Two small bugs were fixed here** with verifiers (follow-up 2026-10-18: F5 and the F8 bundle are now also in code, see their rows): a lost acknowledgement dropped the nine extra reveals of a hatch page (BD-609), and three test-grant remotes existed on every published server although they can only be switched on by a flag that is pinned off (BD-610).

## 5. Findings in detail

**F1 movement envelope (BD-611).** `Config.MovementGuardSpeedTolerance = 3` and `MovementGuardBurstStuds = 4` are PROVISIONAL numbers chosen to cover the jump carry and lag (the Config comment says so). The guard compares each 0.1 s step with `speed x dt x 3 + 4`, then promotes the new position to the baseline, so a client that moves 10.6 studs every tick passes the guard forever (it only gets food while it stays under the 5.5 studs per 0.08 s tick of `FoodService`, about 69 studs/s). `FoodService` and the crystal loop have their own, tighter checks (tolerance 2, burst 2) but over shorter ticks. Offline numbers for `WalkSpeed` 22: guard 106 studs/s, food 69, crystals 52; the harness checks that `MovementEnvelope.plausible` really accepts a step just inside and refuses one just outside. Reward effect: catches are proportional to food eaten, so a cheater at about 65 studs/s collects up to about 3 times faster until the 150-egg run cap, the food density or the respawn timer bites (travel is only part of the time between pickups, so the real gain is lower). Nothing was exploited in Studio. Proposal: replace the per-tick promotion by a sliding one-second displacement budget (about `speed x 1.35 + 6`), keep a wider bound only inside the dash and leap windows that the server opens itself, and measure honest lag and jump traffic in Studio first (it needs a real second client).

**F1 outcome (BD-611, 2026-10-18, offline).** `tools/verify_movement_speed.py` (CI) models every source of horizontal speed above `WalkSpeed` from the real configs, then runs the real `MovementWindow` against honest and hostile traces. | Vehicles, seats, launch pads, springs, conveyors, `BodyVelocity` | none exist in `src/` | n/a |

Every honest speed is bounded by a number in the repository, including the client-owned ones (`JumpCarry`, leap, dash and glide are small client scripts with constants; the server only has to bound their output). What cannot be bounded offline is **replication jitter** (how stale a replicated position can be against a fresh one), collision push-out and slope steps on a real device.

**Tightened (all three checks, one mechanism).** New pure module `src/shared/MovementWindow.luau`, used by `MovementGuard`, the food pickup and the crystal pickup. Over every span up to `MovementGuardWindowSeconds` (2 s) a character may cover at most `limit x MovementGuardWindowSpeedFactor x (span + MovementGuardWindowJitterSeconds) + MovementGuardWindowSlackStuds` studs, where `limit` is the peak of `MovementSpeedLimit` / `WalkSpeed` over the window plus `MovementGuardLimitHoldSeconds` (a dash, a leap or a potion that just ran out). The named margins are `Config.MovementGuardWindowEnabled` (true; the kill switch), `...WindowSeconds` 2, `...WindowSpeedFactor` 1.4 (the 1.3 jump carry plus 8%; the verifier fails if it drops below 1.05 x `JumpForwardCarry`), `...WindowJitterSeconds` 0.3, `...WindowSlackStuds` 6, `MovementGuardLimitHoldSeconds` 0.5. The per-tick checks are unchanged (they still catch teleports). `MovementGuard` rolls a violator back to the **oldest trusted sample of the window**, so the exploit gains nothing: a 106 studs/s client is rejected after 0.3 s and ends 60 s with about 32 studs of net progress (an honest walk: 1,320). Food and crystals refuse pickups while the window is over budget.

| Limit | Old guard sustained | New window sustained | Old food | Old crystals | New x limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 22 (WalkSpeed) | 106 | 38.4 | 69 | 52 | 1.75 |
| 48.4 (x2.2 potion) | 185 | 80.9 | 122 | 105 | 1.67 |

Margins, measured on the real code with adversarial replication delay (square waves, steps and random delay at the 0.1 s, 0.08 s and 0.25 s cadences): no honest case is rejected at the configured 0.3 s; the largest delay absorbed is 0.72 s walking, 0.54 s bunny hopping, 0.42 s buffed bunny hopping, and the potion-expiry, leap, dash, stop-and-go and sprint-start transitions all pass. **Residual:** a cheat that stays just under the window still moves 1.75 x `WalkSpeed` (instead of 4.8x); closing that needs the measured jitter.

**Left for Studio (acceptance, so BD-611 stays Review / test, not Done).** Two clients in one Studio server (or a published playtest) with simulated lag: record `MovementRejectCount` while one client walks, jumps continuously (bunny hop), uses a leap, a dash and a speed potion for five minutes, and note the worst position staleness. If the count stays 0, the numbers can tighten (`WindowJitterSeconds`, `WindowSlackStuds`, `WindowSpeedFactor`); if it rises, widen them or set `MovementGuardWindowEnabled = false` (the old behaviour returns at once).

**F2 receipts (BD-612).** The six ids are in `Config.DeveloperProducts`. `PaidRandomGate` also reads that table ("the experience sells Robux products") to refuse random crystal items, so the table must stay non-empty while the gate matters. The shop hides the packs when `CrystalPacksEnabled` is false, but a prompt is a client call. If one of those products still exists on the playtest universe and is on sale, an exploiter pays Robux, receives crystals and breaks the "no Robux in the playtest" rule of [55 s9](55-playtest-release-runbook.md). The server must not answer `NotProcessedYet` to such a purchase (the player would be charged and receive nothing), so the fix is on Creator Hub: confirm the products are not on the playtest universe, or are off sale. Owner step, two minutes, in the row.

**F3 equip during a run (BD-613).** `C` opens the Dinos menu at any time and `EquipSpecies` checks ownership but not the run state. The class table makes the choice matter (`speedX`: Fly 1.20, Ground 1.00, Water 0.85, Armored 0.80; Fly glide; Water dash; low-poly size and hit box with the BIG trait). A server guard (`in_run` while `RunState` is Active or Ending, copy "Pick your dino before you play") is three lines in `CollectionService`; it is left as a row because it changes what the Dinos menu does during play and the owner may want "applies at your next run" instead.

**F4 write window (BD-614).** Reproduce: `python tools/verify_remote_fuzz.py --runner <luau> --only storm --storm-seconds 120`.

| Hostile of 12 | Honest requests | Refused `rate_limited` | Hostile writes that landed | Honest run settlement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 228 | 3 (1%) | 78 | committed |
| 2 | 208 | 34 (16%) | 128 | committed |
| 4 | 174 | 71 (41%) | 193 | committed |

After the fix (same command, 120 s storms): refused 0 of 228 (0%), 0 of 208 (0%), 0 of 174 (0%); hostile writes that landed 79 / 127 / 205 (all inside the burst + refill bound); store requests 255 / 278 / 351 over about 150 s with nothing throttled and no queue drop; settlements committed. The storm checks now fail above 2% refused, and fail on any throttle; the new self-test mutant (the fair-share path off) fails the 4-hostile check.

Cause: `admit` rejects everyone once the server-wide share (60 + 10 x players, times 0.6) is full; a hostile account refills 1 write a second, so two accounts fill it. The design protects the DataStore budget and the priority writes (it worked), but it is not fair to a player who is far below their own share. Fix (BD-614, `ProfileRepository.admit`): the server-wide share stays the normal limit, but each player's admitted routine writes are also counted in the same 60 s window (memory only, weak-keyed, no profile field), and a player who has used less than `share / players` is still admitted when the window is full, until the window reaches `Config.WriteFairCeiling` (0.8) of 60 + 10 x players. So the spammer, who is over their own part, is shed first (its own token bucket still applies), honest players keep their part, and at least 20% of the DataStore budget stays for priority writes (renew, settlement, load, purchase). The DataStore ceiling is not raised; lease and ownSession checks are untouched; kill switch `Config.WriteFairShareEnabled = false` restores the old all-or-nothing share. Limits: offline mock of 12 players and one flood pattern; a real DataStore budget, real latency and larger servers are not covered.

**F5 empty runs (BD-615).** `commitRunSettlement` always wrote, even for 0 catches (it records the run id for exactly-once). Measured: 6 Start and Home cycles in 34 virtual seconds, 7 writes, nothing earned. With 12 players cycling this is about 2.5 writes a second against a 3 a second budget. **Fixed 2026-10-18 (offline):** a run with 0 eggs, 0 crystals, 0 nests, 0 XP and no egg events is settled in memory, remembering the run id for the session, when the stored profile would not change anyway. The settlement transaction is also a time tick (it moves the run-egg bank into the queue and starts the next nest), so a profile with banked eggs, pending grants or a nest that would advance keeps its write; so does any state other than `Loaded`. Same answer to the caller and the same `LastRun*` values; the only stored difference is the missing ledger entry (`recentOperations`, `settledRuns`, `lastCheckpoint`, none of which is read for an empty run). Now 1 write in 6 cycles (the equip). No field added; the profile golden test is unchanged. Not measured with `verify_load` or on a server.

**F6 objective rewards (BD-616).** Behind `ObjectivesEnabled`. `{action = "seen", id = "fusion_menu"}` pays T9 for a client statement (once; needs one dino). D5 "Add a dino toward a fusion step" (40 crystals a day) is credited by a `quote` for a full owned donor set, which is free and unlimited (20 a window). Both are bounded by the 600-crystal daily cap. **F7 legacy mutation (BD-617).** `MutateSpecies` spends 50 Base or 50 Gold, so it is never cheaper than the ladder, and the client's old Collection panel still has the button. The identity sidecar reconciles by deleting the lowest ids of the plain bucket; it protects fused results and dinosaurs with cosmetics, not `locked` or `favorited` ones (no UI sets them yet; BD-149..151 add a list). **F8 hygiene (BD-618), fixed 2026-10-18 offline except where marked.** The list is in the row: (a) `ClassGlide` stores a flag nobody reads; (b) `ClientWorldReady` is believed; (c) the `PrivateState` hello costs a full snapshot every 0.5 s; (d) `EquipSpecies` re-renders for `already_equipped`; (e) public attributes `IsAdmin`, `LastPredatorUserId` (the user who ate you) and `LastNestClaimAt` (the server's `os.clock()`) say more than the UI needs; (f) limiters keyed by Player object reset on a rejoin (`FeedbackService`, `FusionService`, `ObjectiveService`, `ProgressionService`, the exit cooldown; BD-363 moved the important ones to UserId); (g) the demo `place` numbers; (h) the 50-entry operation ring; (i) `SkinService` (BD-456, flag off) wrote through `applySkin` without `admit`, so a skin toggle was limited only by its own 10-per-10-s window and not counted in the server-wide share.

What changed: (a) the `ClassGlide` handler and its two tables are gone (the RemoteEvent stays as the marker the client waits for, the client stops firing it); (b) `ClientWorldReady` is believed once and only after `IslandTerrainReady`; (c) hello floor 2 s with one trailing snapshot, so a resync is never lost; (d) `already_equipped` returns before `refreshForPlayer`; (e) `LastPredatorUserId` is server memory (`RunLifecycle.lastPredatorUserId`), `LastNestClaimAt` is a per-player counter; **`IsAdmin` is left public**: the admin panel and the HUD debug line read it as a Player attribute, and moving it to `PrivateState` is a client change that cannot be checked without Studio (the information is a display hint; the experience creator is public anyway); (f) the Home cooldown, the Fusion and Objective windows, the Progression 0.4 s gap and the Feedback session limits are keyed by UserId (memory only); (g) `placeOnGround` refuses NaN, infinite and absurd numbers; (h) purchase tokens are also kept in memory, 400 per user, no profile change; (i) `applySkin` calls `admit`.

## 6. What was tried and held

* Every argument of every handler with the full corpus: no throw, no state change that a valid call would not make.
* Reward authority: no handler takes a quantity, price, rarity, species, time, position or id of another player from the client; `grant` is not reachable; run catches come only from the server's food loop; egg rewards are precommitted at settlement.
* Idempotency: purchase, mutation, fusion, objective and run tokens survive 40-way replays and lost acknowledgements; a settlement, Home, death and leave in one tick commit once.
* Isolation: another player's egg, nest, instance and variant ids are refused; no remote answer reached another player; no private value rides a Player attribute.
* Debug paths: with every flag as shipped, a published server has no test-grant, QA, combat, objective, gift, art-preview or demo remote; `DemoTrigger` is absent even with its flag forced on; `AdminAction` refuses a stranger.
* Rollback ([55 s8](55-playtest-release-runbook.md)): this PR adds no profile field and changes no schema; the hatch-page fix only changes what is held in memory, so a rollback to the previous tag loads the same store. The BD-615 and BD-618 follow-up also adds no field (empty runs write less, the other items keep their state in memory); `tools/verify_profile_golden.py` and `tools/verify_rollback.py` are unchanged.
