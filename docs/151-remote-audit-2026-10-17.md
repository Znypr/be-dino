# 151 Remote and server-entry audit, as an exploiter sees it (BD-608, 2026-10-17)

Label: **CURRENT BUILD BEHAVIOR** audit of main `18a9d665` (after about 80 PRs on 2026-10-17), **offline only**. The real `src/server` code ran inside the Luau test harness with a mocked DataStore (latency, the per-server request budget, failed writes, lost acknowledgements). Nothing was run in Studio, on a published server or on a phone, and nothing here is a statement about Roblox networking, client physics or real DataStore timing. Sources read: [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), [145](145-session1-readiness-2026-10-17.md), [55 s8](55-playtest-release-runbook.md) (rollback), [111](111-multiplayer-audit.md), [112](112-private-state.md), [13](13-persistence-remote-contracts.md), [22](22-security-multiplayer-test.md), and every file in `src/server`.

## 1. Result in one paragraph

**No path was found that gives a player free currency, items or another player's data.** Every one of the 29 remote handlers (18 on a published playtest server, 11 more behind flags that are off or Studio only), the receipt callback and the pedestal prompt validates its arguments, takes rewards, ids and time from the server (a character's position is the client's: F1), and ends in an idempotent, lease-checked transaction; 2,255 slot-by-slot hostile calls, 51,425 hostile argument pairs, 40-way parallel replays, lost acknowledgements, three random 260-request sequences on a failing store and floods from up to four clients produced **no error, no corrupted profile and no value created**. What the audit did find is the layer around that: **3 P1** (movement checks accept 3 to 5 times normal speed; a purchase of a Robux crystal pack is honoured even though the packs are switched off; a dinosaur can be swapped in the middle of a run), **4 P2** (a few hostile clients can make other players' routine writes fail; empty runs cost a write each; two objective rewards need no real action; the old 50-copy mutation is still live) and a **P3** bundle. **Two small bugs were fixed here** with verifiers: a lost acknowledgement dropped the nine extra reveals of a hatch page (BD-609), and three test-grant remotes existed on every published server although they can only be switched on by a flag that is pinned off (BD-610).

Remotes are in `ReplicatedStorage.BeDinoRemotes`.

## 5. Findings in detail

**F1 movement envelope (BD-611).** `Config.MovementGuardSpeedTolerance = 3` and `MovementGuardBurstStuds = 4` are PROVISIONAL numbers chosen to cover the jump carry and lag (the Config comment says so). The guard compares each 0.1 s step with `speed x dt x 3 + 4`, then promotes the new position to the baseline, so a client that moves 10.6 studs every tick passes forever. `FoodService` and the crystal loop have their own, tighter checks (tolerance 2, burst 2) but over shorter ticks. Offline numbers for `WalkSpeed` 22: guard 106 studs/s, food 69, crystals 52; the harness checks that `MovementEnvelope.plausible` really accepts a step just inside and refuses one just outside. Reward effect: catches are proportional to food eaten, so up to about 3 times the intended egg rate until the 150-egg run cap, the food density or the respawn timer bites. Nothing was exploited in Studio. Proposal: replace the per-tick promotion by a sliding one-second displacement budget (about `speed x 1.35 + 6`), keep a wider bound only inside the dash and leap windows that the server opens itself, and measure honest lag and jump traffic in Studio first (it needs a real second client).

**F2 receipts (BD-612).** The six ids are in `Config.DeveloperProducts`. `PaidRandomGate` also reads that table ("the experience sells Robux products") to refuse random crystal items, so the table must stay non-empty while the gate matters. The shop hides the packs when `CrystalPacksEnabled` is false, but a prompt is a client call. If one of those products still exists on the playtest universe and is on sale, an exploiter pays Robux, receives crystals and breaks the "no Robux in the playtest" rule of [55 s9](55-playtest-release-runbook.md). The server must not answer `NotProcessedYet` to such a purchase (the player would be charged and receive nothing), so the fix is on Creator Hub: confirm the products are not on the playtest universe, or are off sale. Owner step, two minutes, in the row.

**F3 equip during a run (BD-613).** `C` opens the Dinos menu at any time and `EquipSpecies` checks ownership but not the run state. The class table makes the choice matter (`speedX`: Fly 1.20, Ground 1.00, Water 0.85, Armored 0.80; Fly glide; Water dash; low-poly size and hit box with the BIG trait). A server guard (`in_run` while `RunState` is Active or Ending, copy "Pick your dino before you play") is three lines in `CollectionService`; it is left as a row because it changes what the Dinos menu does during play and the owner may want "applies at your next run" instead.

**F4 write window (BD-614).** Reproduce: `python tools/verify_remote_fuzz.py --runner <luau> --only storm --storm-seconds 120`.

| Hostile of 12 | Honest requests | Refused `rate_limited` | Hostile writes that landed | Honest run settlement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 228 | 3 (1%) | 78 | committed |
| 2 | 208 | 34 (16%) | 128 | committed |
| 4 | 174 | 71 (41%) | 193 | committed |

Cause: `admit` rejects everyone once the server-wide share (60 + 10 x players, times 0.6) is full; a hostile account refills 1 write a second, so two accounts fill it. The design protects the DataStore budget and the priority writes (it worked), but it is not fair to a player who is far below their own share. Proposal: count admitted writes per player inside the window and let a player under `share / players` through even when the window is full.

**F5 empty runs (BD-615).** `commitRunSettlement` always writes, even for 0 catches (it records the run id for exactly-once). Measured: 6 Start and Home cycles in 34 virtual seconds, 7 writes, nothing earned. With 12 players cycling this is about 2.5 writes a second against a 3 a second budget. Proposal: settle a 0-catch run in memory (remember the run id for the session) and write only when something was earned, or keep the write but require a minimum run time before Home counts.

**F6 objective rewards (BD-616).** Behind `ObjectivesEnabled`. `{action = "seen", id = "fusion_menu"}` pays T9 for a client statement (once; needs one dino). D5 "Add a dino toward a fusion step" (40 crystals a day) is credited by a `quote` for a full owned donor set, which is free and unlimited (20 a window). Both are bounded by the 600-crystal daily cap. Decide before the flag goes on: pay D5 on a committed fusion, and let T9 be the only honour-system reward or drop it.

**F7 legacy mutation (BD-617).** `MutateSpecies` spends 50 Base or 50 Gold, so it is never cheaper than the ladder, and the client's old Collection panel still has the button. The identity sidecar reconciles by deleting the lowest ids of the plain bucket; it protects fused results and dinosaurs with cosmetics, not `locked` or `favorited` ones (no UI sets them yet; BD-149..151 add a list). Remove the remote and the old button, or refuse while `IdentityFusionPreviewEnabled` is on.

**F8 hygiene (BD-618).** The list is in the row: (a) `ClassGlide` stores a flag nobody reads; (b) `ClientWorldReady` is believed; (c) the `PrivateState` hello costs a full snapshot every 0.5 s; (d) `EquipSpecies` re-renders for `already_equipped`; (e) public attributes `IsAdmin`, `LastPredatorUserId` (the user who ate you) and `LastNestClaimAt` (the server's `os.clock()`) say more than the UI needs; (f) limiters keyed by Player object reset on a rejoin (`FeedbackService`, `FusionService`, `ObjectiveService`, `ProgressionService`, the exit cooldown; BD-363 moved the important ones to UserId); (g) the demo `place` numbers; (h) the 50-entry operation ring; (i) `SkinService` (BD-456, flag off) writes through `applySkin` without `admit`, so a skin toggle is limited only by its own 10-per-10-s window and is not counted in the server-wide share.

## 6. What was tried and held

* Every argument of every handler with the full corpus: no throw, no state change that a valid call would not make.
* Reward authority: no handler takes a quantity, price, rarity, species, time, position or id of another player from the client; `grant` is not reachable; run catches come only from the server's food loop; egg rewards are precommitted at settlement.
* Idempotency: purchase, mutation, fusion, objective and run tokens survive 40-way replays and lost acknowledgements; a settlement, Home, death and leave in one tick commit once.
* Isolation: another player's egg, nest, instance and variant ids are refused; no remote answer reached another player; no private value rides a Player attribute.
* Debug paths: with every flag as shipped, a published server has no test-grant, QA, combat, objective, gift, art-preview or demo remote; `DemoTrigger` is absent even with its flag forced on; `AdminAction` refuses a stranger.
